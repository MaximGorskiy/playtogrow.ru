For parents

Play to Grow for parents

A parent does not turn every task into a transaction. Their role is to agree on clear rules, notice real effort, and consistently keep promises.

In briefClear rules, calm review, and agreed rewards.

What the parent does

  • Offer several suitable quests instead of assigning one with no choice.
  • Agree on point costs and family limits before the quest begins.
  • Review the specific result instead of comparing the child with others.
  • Confirm only a reward that was actually delivered.
  • Discuss progress and difficulties with a psychologist.

When to contact a professional

Frequent arguments about games are not a diagnosis by themselves. If gaming noticeably displaces sleep, education, relationships, or other important parts of life, or continues despite serious negative consequences, discuss the situation with a qualified psychologist or doctor.

First contact

For a first conversation, say that you are a parent, give the child's age range without their name, and briefly describe the difficulties related to gaming. Do not send diagnoses, medical records, or other sensitive information through public contact channels.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
