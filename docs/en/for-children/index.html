Play to Grow for children and teenagers

You choose real-life quests and log your in-game achievements. A confirmed result can earn points that you use to request a reward agreed in advance.

In briefYou can see the goals and rules, choose the next step, and expect adults to support you and keep the agreement.

What you do

  1. Look through the available real-life quests and the game plan.
  2. Choose a quest or complete a suitable stage in a game.
  3. Write a short note about the result and log the actual time for an in-game result.
  4. Earn the published number of points after the result is reviewed.
  5. Choose an available reward and send a request to your parent.

If the plan is not completed

You can choose an extra real-life quest, ask for help, try a suggested easier stage, or discuss another option. Earned points are not removed and there are no hidden penalties.

Important

The demo does not connect to your game and cannot see your gaming account. Do not enter your real full name, address, school, passwords, or other personal information.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
