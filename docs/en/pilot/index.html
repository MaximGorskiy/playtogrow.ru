Pilot stage

Joining the Play to Grow pilot

We are looking for the first families and psychologists to test whether the workflow is clear, agreements are workable, and the platform is developing in the right direction.

In briefVoluntary participation, minimal data, and honest limitations.

Who we are looking for

  • Parents of a child or teenager when gaming has become a constant source of conflict or displaces important activities.
  • Psychologists for professional support and discussion of the method and ethical boundaries.
  • Families willing to discuss rules and provide specific feedback.

How participation begins

  1. A short introduction without sending documents or detailed personal data.
  2. A discussion of expectations, boundaries, and whether the pilot fits the family.
  3. Agreement on a small set of quests and rewards.
  4. Testing the workflow and regularly reviewing difficulties.
  5. A decision to continue, adjust, or stop participation.

Funding

During the pilot, participating parents contribute according to their means. All funds go to development, infrastructure, and refinement of the method. The author takes no personal profit before the platform is launched.

What we do not promise

Participation does not guarantee reduced gaming time, the disappearance of conflict, or a clinical outcome. The pilot may stop if the format is not suitable for the child or family.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
