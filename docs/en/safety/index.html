Safety and data

Safety, ethics, and data

The public website introduces the idea. Viewing it and using the demo do not require a child's real personal data.

In briefDemo data stays in localStorage for the current browser and domain.

What the current demo stores

  • A nickname and sample quest content if the user changes them.
  • Reports, points, rewards, and settings in browser localStorage.
  • A separate copy on every device, browser, and domain.

What not to enter

  • The child's full legal name.
  • Address, school, schedule, or precise location.
  • Medical documents, diagnoses, or professional reports.
  • Passwords or gaming-account credentials.
  • Photos or details that identify the child.

Technical limitations

The role switch in the demo is not authentication. Browser tools can change the data; it is not synchronized and is deleted when site storage is cleared. The website does not connect to games or control devices.

Future production system

Real accounts, access control, and personal-data processing require a separate backend, consent and retention rules, managed deletion, an audit log, and legal review. These capabilities are not part of the public demo.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
