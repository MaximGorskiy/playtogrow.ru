About Play to Grow

Play to Grow connects useful real-life actions chosen by a child with gaming rewards agreed by the family in advance.

In briefA pilot for families and psychologists, not a finished medical program.

Project purpose

The goal is not to fight the child's interest in games, but to use an existing source of motivation for learning, reading, movement, creativity, relationships, and independence.

The child chooses from suitable quests. After completing one, they share the result; a parent reviews the report and provides the reward agreed in advance.

Three participants

  • The child chooses a quest, shares the result, earns points, and requests a reward.
  • The parent reviews the report, confirms actual reward delivery, and takes part in the family agreement.
  • A moderator with a psychologist's professional knowledge selects quests, analyzes progress, and adjusts the plan.

Current stage

The current release is a landing page and a static three-role demo in one browser. We are looking for the first families and psychologists to test the workflow and refine the method.

Play to Grow does not diagnose, replace a doctor or psychotherapist, or promise a guaranteed outcome.

Discuss participation or collaboration
