Frequently asked questions

Play to Grow questions and answers

Short, concrete answers about the current version of the project.

In briefThe demo, pilot, and limitations without marketing promises.

Does Play to Grow treat gaming addiction?

No. It is currently a pilot and a demonstration. It does not diagnose, replace a doctor or psychotherapist, or promise a treatment outcome.

Does a child have to stop gaming or follow a time cap?

No. The current model does not block play on a timer. The child logs actual play time manually, and a psychologist considers it alongside sleep, education, movement, relationships, and wellbeing.

What quests does a child receive?

Quests may cover learning, reading, movement, creativity, relationships, and independence. The specific set is developed for the family with a psychologist.

Who chooses the quest?

The child chooses from several suitable options. They can ask for help, suggest their own quest, or discuss a replacement.

Can a child earn points for an in-game win?

Yes. A psychologist or moderator sets a visible game plan. The child logs an achievement, an adult reviews it, and the published number of points is awarded after approval. Minutes alone do not earn points.

What happens if the game plan is not completed?

The child is offered a voluntary extra real-life quest, support, or an agreed easier stage. Earned points are not removed and there are no hidden penalties.

What rewards can be used?

An in-game bonus or currency, playing together, or another agreed opportunity. The family sets the point cost, weekly budget, and delivery rules in advance.

Does the platform deliver game currency?

No. The site does not connect to gaming accounts or buy anything. A parent provides the agreed reward independently.

What does the psychologist do?

They help clarify goals, select observable quests, analyze progress, and change the plan if it is unhelpful or increases conflict.

Is child data stored on a server?

The current public demo has no server database. Demo data stays in localStorage for the specific browser and domain.

Should real child information be entered?

No. Use fictional data and do not enter a full name, address, school, documents, diagnoses, photos, or passwords.

How can I join the pilot?

Contact the author through Telegram, email, or phone. For a first conversation, simply say whether you are a parent or professional and briefly state your question.

How is parent funding used?

During the pilot, all contributions go to development, infrastructure, and refinement of the method. The author takes no personal profit before launch.

Can the roles use different devices?

Not yet. The current version demonstrates three roles in one browser. Shared accounts and synchronization require a separate protected backend.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
