Play to Grow updates

Public changes to the product, method, and safety rules.

In briefChanges relevant to families and professionals are recorded here.
30 October 2026

In-game progress instead of a time cap

The demo now includes a visible weekly game plan, manual achievement and play-time logging, review of in-game results, and points for confirmed achievements. The hard time limit was removed; minutes are used only as context. Missing the plan leads to an optional extra quest without hidden penalties.

30 October 2026

Bilingual pages and open indexing

Separate Russian and English pages were added for parents, children, and psychologists, together with the method, safety, pilot, and FAQ. Sitemaps, crawler rules, and machine-readable project information were added.

26 October 2026

Two synchronized public releases

Both domains were synchronized, Russian remained the default language, and English stayed available through the language switch. Demo role navigation and the return to the start page were clarified.
