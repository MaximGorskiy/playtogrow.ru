I am looking for patients and families affected by computer and gaming addiction

At this first stage, I am looking for parents with a child or teenager whose gaming causes conflict or displaces school, movement, relationships, or other important activities. Together, we will test whether familiar gaming motivation can help restore interest in real-life growth.

How the idea works

Instead of taking games away, make them a bridge to growth and mutual understanding. What each participant gains:

Real-life quests, an in-game progress plan, and reward criteria are agreed in advance and remain visible to the child. Play time is logged as context, not used as a hard limit or a source of points by itself.

Three participants — one team

Each participant has specific actions in the system

01

Child

Chooses real-life quests, logs in-game achievements and play time, earns points for confirmed results, and requests an agreed gaming reward.

02

Parent

Confirms the report, confirms delivery of the reward, and contributes financially to the project's development according to the family's means.

03

Moderator

Adds a psychologist's expertise to the quests, sets a visible in-game progress plan, suggests related games or easier stages, and reviews progress in context.

An honest note on the project stage

This is a pilot, not a finished medical program

We are gathering feedback, refining the method, and testing scenarios with families and psychologists. The platform does not diagnose, replace a doctor or psychotherapist, or promise guaranteed outcomes.

All funds go only toward developing the project
