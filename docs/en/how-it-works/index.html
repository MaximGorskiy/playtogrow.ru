The working cycle

How Play to Grow works

The goal, quest, and reward are discussed in advance. The child keeps a choice, adults notice the effort, and a psychologist helps analyze progress.

In briefChoice → action → report → review → reward → analysis.

Six steps

  1. The family and psychologist choose a development area.
  2. The child chooses one of the available quests.
  3. The child completes it and submits a short report.
  4. A parent approves the result or calmly returns it for an update.
  5. After approval, the child earns points and requests an agreed gaming reward.
  6. The psychologist analyzes progress and adjusts future quests with the family.

What can be a reward

It may be extra game time, an in-game bonus or currency, playing together, or another opportunity that matters to the child. The family decides in advance which rewards are acceptable and how they are delivered.

What the platform does not do

  • It does not buy game currency automatically.
  • It does not connect to the child's gaming accounts.
  • It does not measure or block screen time.
  • It does not replace a professional assessment of the child's condition.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
