For psychologists

Play to Grow for psychologists

A professional helps turn the general idea into an individualized, observable, and ethically appropriate process for a specific family.

In briefWe invite professional dialogue, critical review, and pilot collaboration.

Proposed role

  • Clarify the family's request and functional difficulties without diagnosing through the website.
  • Select observable quests and completion criteria.
  • Help the family agree on rewards that do not intensify conflict or financial risk.
  • Analyze progress, reasons for setbacks, and the need to adjust the plan.
  • Identify situations where this format is unsuitable and different support is needed.

What we want to test

The relevant outcomes are not simply the number of rewards delivered. We want to examine sustained participation, initiative in real life, the quality of family agreements, and whether conflict is avoided rather than intensified.

Invitation to collaborate

We are looking for psychologists who work with children, teenagers, families, or problematic gaming behavior. We welcome critical review of the wording, discussion of ethical boundaries, and joint development of a pilot protocol.

Discuss participation or collaboration

A first message does not require documents or a child's personal data.
